Our Story: Built for Scale, Built for You

The journey of mysolar.id began with a consortium of a strategic partnership, who are capable of handling massive volume—from single-family homes to large-scale industrial projects across Indonesia.

We realized that many people wanted to switch to clean energy but were held back by the complexity and the upfront costs. Because of our partner’s unique ability to supply and install in massive amounts, we are able to offer a revolutionary approach to solar.

Whether you are looking to power your family home or a massive industrial site, we have the capacity to deliver. We are here to make solar energy accessible, affordable, and simple for every Indonesian property owner. Our mission is to lead the way toward a brighter, more sustainable Indonesia, one roof at a time.
