The Old Way: Our Reliance on Fossil Fuels

For decades, our electrical grids have been heavily reliant on burning fossil fuels, primarily coal and natural gas. While this system has powered our modern world, it comes with a steep cost. Burning fossil fuels releases massive amounts of carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases into the atmosphere, driving climate change and polluting the air we breathe. Furthermore, fossil fuels are finite resources subject to volatile global market prices, which inevitably leads to rising electricity costs for homes and businesses.

What is a Solar Panel?

A solar panel is a device that captures sunlight and converts it directly into electricity. It’s a clean, silent, and incredibly efficient way to generate your own power. By placing these panels on your roof, you are essentially turning your home or building into a private power plant.
